## The entry

5 characters, exactly one word, plain ASCII — inside the 1–77 character gate.

## Why this word

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **One real word** — a word every reader recognises, spelled correctly, nothing added around it.
2. **Deletion funny at first sight** — the joke lands the moment the word is gone, with no caption or defence needed.
3. **Humour from the word itself** — its sound alone makes the room wince before any meaning arrives; the relief of erasing it is immediate.
4. **Fresh** — avoids the corporate-jargon picks like "synergy" that the rest of the field will reach for first.
5. **Exact** — one word, spelled correctly, with nothing added around it.
